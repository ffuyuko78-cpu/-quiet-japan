🏛️ 迎賓館赤坂離宮 — Tokyo State Guest House

Built in 1909 during the Meiji era, the Akasaka Palace is Japan's only Western-style palace — a rare Neo-Baroque masterpiece in the heart of Tokyo. For over a century, it has welcomed world leaders and heads of state, serving as the official state guest house since 1974. Today, visitors can stroll its serene gardens, admire the ornate ironwork gates, and feel the quiet weight of history.

✈ For Visitors: Open to the public on select days. As of January 2025: gardens ¥300, main building ¥2,000. A 10-minute walk from Yotsuya or Shinanomachi Station. Best in winter when the garden is calm and uncrowded — exactly as these photos were taken. ※ Admission fees and opening schedules may change — always check the official website before visiting.

明治42年（1909年）に建てられた迎賓館赤坂離宮は、日本唯一のネオ・バロック様式の宮殿です。東京の中心に佇むこの壮麗な建物は、100年以上にわたって世界各国の要人を迎えてきました。1974年からは国の迎賓館として使用され、現在は一般公開も行われています。白い外壁、精緻な鉄細工の門、松が整然と並ぶ庭園——静かな格調の中に、日本の近代史が刻まれています。
